ICT Club Certificate Verification Portal

The ICT Club is the only officially recognized outstanding student club of MNS-UET Multan. Each event, seminar, or activity conducted by the ICT Club awards participating students with officially issued certificates. These certificates carry a unique verification code that students, employers, or institutions can verify here to validate authentic engagement and participation.
